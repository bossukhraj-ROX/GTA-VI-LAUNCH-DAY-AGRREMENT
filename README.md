GTA VI Launch Day Agreement

An independent, fan-made novelty agreement created for the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI.

About the Project

The GTA VI Launch Day Agreement is a digital novelty product built around the anticipation of one of gaming’s most awaited releases.

The agreement uses formal contract-style wording, humorous rules and signature fields to turn launch-day excitement into a shareable experience.

Product

* Product: GTA VI Launch Day Agreement
* Format: Digital PDF
* Price: £10
* Type: One-time purchase
* Signature detail: A clause referencing the 13-year wait for the game

Website
